GhostPin

GhostPin is a free Mac app giving you total control over your iPhone's GPS over USB—no jailbreak or VPNs needed.
Use the interactive map to teleport instantly, build multi-stop routes, import GPX files, or simulate walking, biking, and driving.
Unplug your phone to snap back to reality instantly.a
